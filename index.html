Vision, Cameras & Printers

Inspection and traceability, integrated end-to-end

Machine Vision & Camera Systems

  • 2D/3D cameras: Basler, Cognex, Keyence, IFM — installation, calibration, PLC I/O integration.
  • Automated inspection: surface defects, colour mismatch, shape and dimensional tolerance at line speed.
  • Barcode/QR/OCR reading: 1D/2D barcode, Data Matrix and text recognition for full traceability.
  • Vision-guided robotics: pick-and-place, bin picking, seam tracking, palletising.
  • Reject & divert systems: air blast, diverter gates, robot eject.

Industrial Printer Integration

  • Inkjet, laser & thermal: Markem-Imaje, Domino, Videojet, Zebra — installation, PLC interface, speed matching.
  • Variable data printing: date/time, batch no., serial no., expiry, shift code — driven live from PLC or ERP.
  • Labelling & marking: pressure-sensitive labels, heat-shrink sleeves, direct part marking (DPM).
  • Print verification: camera-based check after print, auto-reject on misprint.
  • ERP/MES integration: SAP, Oracle, or custom MES for real-time variable data.
