HMI → SCADA Upgradation

From isolated HMIs to plant-wide visibility

Before — Legacy HMI Challenges
Isolated Panels

Each machine has its own standalone HMI — no data sharing between lines.

No Remote Access

Engineers must physically visit the machine floor for any monitoring task.

Limited Alarms

Basic on/off alarms only; no priority levels, escalation or history.

Manual Reports

Operators write shift reports by hand leading to errors and delays.

No Data Trending

No historical logging — impossible to analyse process drift over time.

After — AIO SCADA Solution
Centralised SCADA

All machines networked to one SCADA server — plant-wide visibility on a single screen.

Web & Mobile Access

Monitor live production KPIs from office, home or smartphone 24/7.

Advanced Alarm Mgmt

Priority-based alarms, auto-escalation emails/SMS, full alarm history.

Automated Reports

Shift, batch, daily & OEE reports auto-emailed to management on schedule.

Full Historian & Recipe Management

Every process variable logged; recipes stored and downloaded digitally.
